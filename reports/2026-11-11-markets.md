# Which Bullet market to make with $100? (2026-11-11)

**Short answer: none of them makes real money with this strategy yet.** The
least-bad choice is **HYPE-USD at 10 bp**, with $10 orders (0.14 HYPE) and a
max position of 0.28 HYPE (about $20). Expect about $0 to $0.15 a day,
which is noise on $100. It is worth running only to learn, in dry run first.

## What was run

- **Data.** 24 same-asset Bullet markets, recorded 2026-11-10 from 02:55 to
  24:00 UTC (19–21 h), each against its Binance USD-M perp tape (fair price =
  perp trade + learned gap). Fees are 0, as on Bullet.
- **Sizing for $100.** Orders of about $10, rounded up to each market's lot
  (`screen --order-usd 10`, configs in `data/configs`). Max position is 2
  orders. Latency is 25 ms.
- **REAL.** A replay of the Bullet recording at 1/2/5/10/20 bp.
- **PROXY.** The same day, with Bullet imitated from the perp tape using the
  freshly calibrated profile, at seeds 1–3, to check the proxy against REAL.
- **30-day PROXY** (2026-10-10 to 2026-11-09). Run only for the 8 least-bad
  markets, at 10 and 20 bp, where REAL and PROXY are closest.

Each market's reports are in `reports/2026-11-11-markets/<MARKET>/`. The
screen table is in `reports/market-screen-2026-11-10.md`.

## What the day says

1. **Tight quotes get picked off everywhere.** At 1–2 bp every market loses:
   SOL −$10/day, HYPE −$14, ZEC −$43, LIT −$38 at 2 bp. The +10 s markout
   is −1 to −3 bp per fill, bigger than the half-spread we earn. Someone
   trading on Bullet sees the move before our Binance-based fair price does.
2. **Wide quotes barely trade.** At 10–20 bp fills fall to 0–3 an hour. Pnl
   is between −$1 and +$0.7 a day, on too few fills to tell skill from luck.
3. **Most markets have no real flow.** Apart from SOL ($192k/h), ZEC
   ($108k/h) and HYPE ($78k/h), every market trades about 180–230 times an
   hour for $0.4–5k/h. That is roughly Bullet's ~20 s bot trade and little
   else, so there is no one to make a market to.
4. **Some reference prices go stale.** In BNC (3%), UNITREE (11%), RAY (21%),
   TIA (24%) and SILVER (30%), the bot quotes both sides only a small part of
   the day. The Binance perp is often silent for more than 5 s, so the bot
   pulls its quotes.
5. **The proxy is still kinder than reality.** At 1–2 bp it loses only about
   40–70% of what REAL loses, and its markouts are about 0.5–1 bp better. So
   treat the 30-day numbers below as an upper bound.

## Ranking for $100

1. **HYPE-USD, 10 bp, 0.14 per order, max 0.28.** It has the most
   consistent proxy (22/31 positive days, drawdown $1.5), real flow on Bullet
   ($78k/h, not only the bot), the perp reference is rarely stale (quotes 90%
   of the day), and REAL on 11-10 was slightly positive. At 1.2 fills an hour
   that day, it is still close to nothing.
2. **SOL-USD, 10 bp, 0.12 per order, max 0.24.** It has the deepest flow and
   the smallest drawdowns, and REAL was +$0.33/day on 11-10. But it got
   almost no fills that day (0.9/h), because Bullet's own SOL spread is 0.7 bp
   and our 10 bp quotes are far behind it.
3. **PUMP / SPCX / RAY at 10 bp.** These are positive in the proxy, but most
   of their Bullet flow is the bot, and RAY's reference is often stale.

**Avoid:** ZEC, LIT, NEAR, ENA, UNI, SNDK, NVDA. They are picked off hard at
every spread that trades. **Avoid** TIA, BNC and UNITREE too: the TIA proxy
swings from −$8 to +$12 depending on the spread, and BNC and UNITREE barely
quote.

## Caveats

- There is only **one real day**. The REAL pnl at 10–20 bp rests on 5–60
  fills: noise.
- The proxy is optimistic by about 0.5–1 bp of markout, which is most of the
  30-day profit.
- Real queue position is unknown: the default fills only on trade-through,
  which assumes we're behind everyone at our price.
- Funding (Bullet charges a 5% funding spread) is not modelled. With a ±$20
  position it is cents.
- Even if the best case is right, $0.13/day on $100 is about 4%/month at
  best. The bigger win would be cutting adverse selection (a faster or better
  fair price) rather than picking a market.
