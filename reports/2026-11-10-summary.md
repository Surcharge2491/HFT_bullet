## Caveats
- PROXY, not Bullet: the 5 bp profit comes from the stand-in Bullet's price
  wandering away from Binance and back (measured: ±1.6 bp, half-life 15 s) and
  crossing our quotes. Whether real Bullet does that, rather than moving away
  for a reason, only REAL data can tell.
- Edge at 5 bp is small per fill; a maker fee of about 1 bp or more likely
  wipes it out.

Fill-at-touch changes little on REAL (5 bp identical; 2 bp 627 fills, −$0.32; 1 bp 1332, −$0.71).
With the perp reference the bot quotes 96% of the time (vs 5% with the spot SOLUSDC reference).

**What it means**

- At 5 bp, real Bullet filled us 4 times in 2 h; the proxy fills 10–15× more.
  The 3-month 5 bp profit ($25) comes from fills the real venue didn't give.
  **Treat it as not validated.** Likely cause: the proxy's random basis
  wander (OU, σ 1.4 bp) moves its book 5 bp away from Binance far more often
  than real Bullet does, and its sampled trades then hit our quotes.
- At 1–2 bp the proxy's fill counts are in the right range and the spot-tape
  proxy matches real adverse selection (≈ −1.1 to −1.4 bp per fill at +10 s).
  The perp-tape proxy is too kind (−0.5 bp).
- REAL loses at 1–2 bp before fees: every fill is picked off by ~1.2 bp and the
  spread we earn is about the same or less. Nothing here is profitable on real
  data yet. 2 h is a small sample; today's ~20 h of recording gets the same
  test tomorrow.

**Next**: fix the proxy's 5 bp overfill (check basis-wander vs real basis
excursions, and trade offsets beyond the touch) before trusting any
3-month PROXY number.
